usage: full_training.py [-h] [--run-dir RUN_DIR] {prepare,smoke,train}

Train the original NN-TENO5 backbone through the full conservative FV solver.
New checkpoints only. Original data/test files and earlier experiments are
read only. A fixed-time training rollout never substitutes a repaired average
or a shorter physical horizon after a failed step. Inference uses bounded
retries.

positional arguments:
  {prepare,smoke,train}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --run-dir RUN_DIR
