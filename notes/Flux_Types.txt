What are the Main families of numerical fluxes ?

  1. Upwind flux: 
    The simplest example appears in linear advection: 
                ut + aux = 0
    if a > 0: 
      information travels from left to right 
      Therefore: f^ = au^L 
    if a < 0: 
      information travels from right to left 
      Therefore: f^ = au^R 

    Advantages: 
      - extremely simple 
      - physically intuitive 
      - excellent for linear advection 

    Limitation: 
      For nonlinear systems such as Euler, there are several waves travelling at different speeds, so the situation the situation is more complicated
